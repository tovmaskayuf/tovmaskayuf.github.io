    Essenta PRO Multi Care Complex

    All-round care

    A balanced formula for complete everyday care of the mouth, combining effective mineral components with natural plant extracts. Enriched with fluoride (Sodium fluoride), zinc citrate (Zinc citrate) and sodium gluconate (Sodium gluconate), the paste helps prevent plaque, protects the mouth and teeth, and gives added protection between brushings. Extracts of ORGANIC peppermint leaf (Mentha piperita leaf extract) and bamboo sap (Bambusa vulgaris sap extract) have antioxidant properties and, combined with menthol (Menthol), provide gentle gum care and fresh breath. Mild, complete care throughout the day. For adults and children aged 7 and over.
